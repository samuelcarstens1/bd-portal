Salary ROI

Gross margin from placements I originated, divided by the fully loaded cost of my position.
Sample data
ROI to date3.1×Target by month 12: 5.0×
Gross margin originated[$ GM]From crews placed on jobs I opened
Fully loaded seat cost[$ cost]Salary + benefits + expenses, to date

How it's calculated

Heads placed × billable hours × margin per hour= Gross margin
Salary + benefits + T&E, prorated to date= Seat cost
Gross margin ÷ Seat cost= ROI

Pipeline value is shown separately and never counted here. Only invoiced work counts toward ROI.
